Cost-Sensitive Machine Learning Pipeline to Minimize Credit Defaults while Maximizing Lending Volume

Project Overview

Built during my Machine Learning Internship at DecodeLabs, this project develops an intelligent credit risk decisioning engine designed to automate loan underwriting. The pipeline predicts whether an applicant will default on a credit facility, optimizing financial trade-offs between underwriting revenue and potential charge-off losses.

By incorporating cost-sensitive learning and asymmetric threshold optimization, the system avoids the common trap of treating credit defaults and good approvals with equal importance, delivering a business-aligned scoring model for lending institutions.

90% Test ROC-AUC 82% Default F1-Score Cost-Sensitive Boundary Financial Risk Guardrail

Business Problem & Financial Trade-Offs

  • Asymmetric Lending Cost: When a financial institution approves a bad loan (False Positive), it risks losing the full principal amount. When it declines a good borrower (False Negative), it only loses the incremental interest income. A standard 0.5 decision threshold fails to account for this 5x–10x loss ratio.
  • Missing Values & Skewed Data: Credit history indicators and income profiles often exhibit high right-skewness and incomplete applicant entries, requiring robust statistical preprocessing.
  • Underwriting Explainability: Credit models must adhere to fair lending standards and provide interpretable feature contributions.

Methodology: Google PACE Framework

  1. Plan: Defined underwriting metrics focused on ROC-AUC and expected portfolio profit. Modeled risk penalties for credit defaults into the validation pipeline.
  2. Analyze: Explored key predictive factors: credit history consistency, combined household income (applicant + coapplicant), loan amount-to-income ratio (DTI proxy), and property location.
  3. Construct:
    • Implemented median/mode imputations for missing credit history and marital status indicators.
    • Applied log/Yeo-Johnson transformations to stabilize heavy-tailed income and loan amounts.
    • Trained and benchmarked Logistic Regression, Support Vector Machines, Random Forest, and XGBoost Classifier.
    • Tuned tree depth, regularization parameters, and subsample ratios via stratified 5-fold cross-validation.
  4. Execute: Analyzed ROC and Precision-Recall trade-off curves to establish a customized operating cutoff that minimizes bad debt exposure while preserving healthy approval volume.

Key Verified Results

  • 90% ROC-AUC Score: The optimized XGBoost model achieved 0.90 area under the ROC curve, significantly outperforming linear benchmarks.
  • 82% Default Prediction F1-Score: Successfully identified high-risk applicants with high precision and strong recall.
  • Actionable Risk Stratification: Generated probabilistic risk scores categorizing applicants into prime, near-prime, and subprime approval tiers.
