Project Overview

Created during my Machine Learning Internship at DecodeLabs, this project delivers an actionable HR intelligence system predicting employee turnover risks before resignations occur. Unplanned employee attrition imposes heavy costs on organizations—ranging from 50% to 200% of an employee's annual salary in recruiting, onboarding, and productivity loss.

Going beyond black-box classification, this project places heavy emphasis on model explainability (XAI) and feature importance analysis, giving people operations teams clear diagnostic reasoning behind why specific individuals or departments exhibit elevated departure risks.

86% Overall Accuracy 0.87 ROC-AUC Flight-Risk Profiling Explainable AI (XAI)

Organizational Problem & Analytical Goals

  • Proactive vs. Reactive HR: Traditional retention efforts happen during exit interviews when it is already too late. A predictive system provides an early-warning horizon (3–6 months ahead).
  • Interpretable Drivers: Business stakeholders reject models that cannot explain their recommendations. Pinpointing whether flight risk is driven by excessive overtime, compensation disparities, or stagnant promotion cycles is critical.
  • Multivariate Demographic & Workplace Data: Incorporates salary, commute distance, work-life balance, job role, marital status, and stock option levels.

Methodology: Google PACE Framework

  1. Plan: Framed the business objective to minimize voluntary attrition among high-performing personnel. Outlined ROC-AUC and Recall on the attrition class as primary optimization targets.
  2. Analyze: Explored bivariate correlations: employees logging overtime were nearly 3x more likely to depart; distance from home showed positive non-linear attrition correlation; stock option levels 0 and 3 exhibited heightened departure rates.
  3. Construct:
    • Engineered composite indicators: tenure-to-age ratio, salary hike-to-satisfaction index, and overtime risk flags.
    • Benchmarked Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, Random Forest, and XGBoost with 10-fold cross-validation.
    • Conducted tree-based feature importance ranking and SHAP value decomposition to explain individual predictions.
  4. Execute: Deployed a scoring pipeline that calculates an individual attrition probability score, segmenting workers into low, moderate, and critical flight-risk categories with tailored intervention recommendations.

Key Verified Findings & Results

  • 86% Accuracy & 0.87 ROC-AUC: High diagnostic discrimination between stable personnel and prospective leavers.
  • Top Attrition Drivers Discovered:
    1. Overtime Working Hours: Single largest factor driving premature turnover.
    2. Monthly Income / Compensation Parity: Critical threshold beneath which retention drops sharply.
    3. Years in Current Role Without Promotion: Plateau effect triggering active external job searching.
  • Actionable HR Playbook: Recommended targeted retention strategies (comp adjustments, role rotations, workload redistribution) to lower annual turnover costs by estimated double-digit percentages.
