Deep NLP: 10 Model Architecture Benchmark

Rigorous Comparison from Classical Recurrent Networks to Fine-Tuned Transformers

Project Overview

Natural Language Processing has evolved rapidly from simple n-gram heuristics and sequential recurrent cells to self-attention mechanisms and large pretrained transformer encoders. For engineering teams, selecting an NLP model requires balancing inference latency, memory footprint, and predictive accuracy. This project systematically benchmarks 10 distinct deep learning architectures under identical conditions on a balanced Amazon customer reviews corpus.

90% Top F1-Score 10 Architectures Compared TensorFlow & HuggingFace

The 10 Benchmarked Architectures

  1. Vanilla RNN: Baseline recurrent neural network testing short-range dependency modeling.
  2. LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory): Gated recurrent unit mitigating vanishing gradients over paragraph-length sequences.
  3. GRU (Gated Recurrent Unit): Compact recurrent architecture with lower parameter count for faster training.
  4. BiLSTM (Bidirectional LSTM): Captures forward and reverse contextual dependencies across the sentence.
  5. BiGRU (Bidirectional GRU): Dual-direction gating optimizing sequence comprehension and throughput.
  6. TextCNN: 1D convolutional network utilizing multi-kernel parallel filters (sizes 3, 4, 5) to capture localized semantic n-grams.
  7. Transformer Encoder (Custom): Multi-head self-attention encoder with positional encodings trained from scratch.
  8. BERT (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers): Pretrained bidirectional masked language model fine-tuned for classification.
  9. DistilBERT: Knowledge-distilled variant of BERT maintaining ~97% of BERT's performance with 40% fewer parameters and 60% faster inference.
  10. T5 (Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer): Unified encoder-decoder transformer framed as sequence generation.

Experimental Setup & Methodology

  • Corpus: Balanced Amazon customer reviews dataset ensuring equal class representation and preventing majority-class distortion.
  • Standardization: Consistent train/validation/test partitions, AdamW optimizer, learning rate warmup with cosine decay, early stopping on validation loss, and identical evaluation metrics.
  • Evaluation Criteria: Macro F1-score, Precision, Recall, Confusion Matrices, Parameter Count, and Inference Latency per sample.

Key Verified Results

  • The best-performing fine-tuned transformer model achieved a 90% F1-score on the held-out test split.
  • TextCNN delivered the highest throughput-to-accuracy ratio among non-pretrained models, outperforming standard LSTMs by 3x on inference speed while remaining competitive on accuracy.
  • DistilBERT matched full BERT accuracy within 1.2% while reducing memory usage by 40%, proving ideal for latency-sensitive production deployment.

What This Project Demonstrates

Rigorous, empirical research discipline in Deep Learning & NLP: moving beyond one-off model training to systematic architecture comparison, trade-off analysis, and production readiness evaluation.
