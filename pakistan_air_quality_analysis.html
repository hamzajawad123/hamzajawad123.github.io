A Statistical Analysis of Pakistan Air Quality (2024–2025)

Assessment of Hazardous Smog: A Nationwide Risk Analysis

Introduction

This project analyzes nationwide AQI data across Pakistan to assess whether smog-related pollution is limited to Lahore or represents a broader public health risk. The objective is to evaluate regional air quality trends and identify nationwide pollution exposure patterns.

Objective

  • Assess daily AQI safety levels across Pakistan.
  • Compare pollution trends between Lahore and other cities.
  • Evaluate the relationship between PM2.5 and AQI levels.
  • Determine whether pollution risk is region-specific or national.

Data Cleaning and Preparation

  1. Compiled AQI records for 2024–2025.
  2. Classified safe and unsafe AQI thresholds.
  3. Segmented data by geographic region.
  4. Calculated mean AQI levels across cities.
  5. Analyzed correlation between PM2.5 and AQI values.

Analysis & Key Findings

  • Approximately 47.86% of days exceeded safe AQI levels.
  • Lahore mean AQI ≈ 246.
  • Rest of Pakistan mean AQI ≈ 247.
  • AQI distributions overlapped significantly across regions.
  • Weak correlation observed between PM2.5 and AQI.

Conclusion

Air pollution risk is statistically similar across Pakistan, indicating that smog is a nationwide issue rather than a Lahore-specific problem. Current regional narratives underestimate the extent of national exposure.

Recommendations

  • Federal Policy: Implement a nationwide clean air policy.
  • Emission Control: Address crop burning and industrial emissions.
  • Strategic Focus: Shift from city-level to national interventions.
  • Public Safety: Launch nationwide AQI alert systems.

Summary

  • Domain Environmental Data Analytics
  • Skills Hypothesis Testing, Correlation Analysis, Data Interpretation
