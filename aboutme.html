Hamza Jawad

Data Scientist & AI-ML Engineer | Data Analyst

BS Data Science at University of Central Punjab (CGPA 3.77) • Building production-style ML pipelines, MLOps systems, and real-time streaming analytics.

Stage 1: The Foundation — Data Analysis

My journey in data began with Data Analysis. Working extensively with SQL, Excel, and Tableau, I developed a strong grounding in handling real-world, imperfect data—learning how to clean datasets thoroughly, conduct exploratory data analysis (EDA), and design interactive dashboards that communicate clear business insights. During projects and remote internships at Mentoga and Excelerate, I learned that high-impact decision-making starts with an honest understanding of data quality and stakeholder needs.

Stage 2: The Evolution — Predictive Modeling & Data Science

As my interest shifted from understanding past metrics to anticipating future patterns, I naturally evolved into Data Science. Expanding deeply into Python, Pandas, NumPy, and Scikit-learn, I focused on statistical modeling, feature engineering, and model evaluation. In my role at DecodeLabs, I built 4 end-to-end ML classification pipelines spanning credit card fraud detection, loan approval, and employee attrition, using techniques like SMOTE for minority class recall and threshold tuning to deliver reliable business recommendations.

Stage 3: Current Direction — AI / ML Systems & MLOps

Today, my primary focus is Machine Learning Engineering and MLOps. At 10Pearls, I built an end-to-end pipeline forecasting Lahore's Air Quality Index at 24, 48, and 72-hour intervals, training LightGBM, XGBoost, and Prophet models, gating them against naive persistence baselines, and serving predictions through a containerized FastAPI backend and Streamlit dashboard. In parallel, I explore deep NLP architectures (benchmarking 10 models from RNNs to BERT and T5) and distributed streaming systems using Apache Kafka and PySpark Structured Streaming.

Data Analysis remains the foundation of how I think: no matter how complex the neural network or pipeline is, it must solve a genuine problem, respect the data, and deliver verifiable results.
