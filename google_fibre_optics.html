Optimizing Customer Support

Reducing Repeat Call Volume using SQL & Tableau

Introduction

This project analyzes customer support interaction logs to identify the root causes of repeat call volume in Internet and WiFi technical support services. The goal is to reduce operational costs and improve customer satisfaction by addressing issues that are not resolved during the first interaction.

Objective

  • Identify support topics causing customers to call back within 7 days.
  • Map raw Problem Type codes into business-related categories.
  • Analyze repeat call trends across different operational markets.
  • Highlight technical issues impacting customer trust and service efficiency.

Data Cleaning and Preparation

  1. Cleaned and queried interaction logs using SQL.
  2. Mapped raw issue codes into categories such as Internet Support and Account Management.
  3. Filtered call records to isolate repeat calls within a 7-day window.
  4. Segmented data by support category and operational market.
  5. Prepared structured datasets for Tableau visualization.

Analysis & Key Findings

  • The majority of repeat calls occur within 24–48 hours after the first interaction.
  • Internet & WiFi issues account for approximately 50% of repeat calls.
  • Technical problems generate 2x more repeat calls than Technician Troubleshooting.
  • Market_1 experiences significant call spikes on Mondays.
  • Market_2 maintains stable and consistently low call volume.

Conclusion

The primary drivers of repeat calls are Internet and WiFi technical failures within Market_1. Unresolved hardware and connectivity issues are creating early-week service backlogs, particularly on Mondays, resulting in recurring customer interactions.

Recommendations

  • Audit Internet Equipment: Evaluate routers and connectivity devices for recurring technical faults.
  • Swarm Team Deployment: Assign additional technical support staff to Market_1 on Mondays.
  • Training Focus: Prioritize Advanced WiFi Troubleshooting over Account Management.
  • First Call Resolution Strategy: Address technical root causes during initial interactions.

Summary

  • Tools Used SQL, Tableau
  • Domain Customer Support Analytics
  • Skills Data Cleaning, Trend Analysis, Insight Generation
