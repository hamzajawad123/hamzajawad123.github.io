Excelerate Learning Path Analysis

Analyzing Learner Funnel & Retention Trends

Introduction

This project analyzes learner progression data across the Excelerate onboarding funnel to identify bottlenecks affecting learner engagement and completion rates. The objective is to understand onboarding delays, wait times, and dropout patterns impacting retention.

Objective

  • Analyze how learners move through the Excelerate funnel.
  • Identify patterns in onboarding delays and inactivity.
  • Examine engagement differences between Courses and Internships.
  • Detect process bottlenecks reducing learner completion.

Data Cleaning and Preparation

  1. Prepared learner progression data using Excel.
  2. Categorized learners based on funnel stages.
  3. Analyzed wait times between application and onboarding.
  4. Segmented engagement levels across Courses and Internships.
  5. Structured datasets for performance trend analysis.

Analysis & Key Findings

  • Out of 8,558 applicants, only 29 learners completed the program.
  • 3,276 learners were allocated but never started.
  • Nearly 80% of active users were waiting for onboarding.
  • Internships had the highest inactivity rate at 78%.
  • Learners waiting 300+ days rarely completed the program.

Conclusion

The primary issue affecting learner completion is onboarding delay. While recruitment remains effective, long wait times between application and start significantly reduce engagement and completion likelihood. Faster onboarding directly increases retention rates.

Recommendations

  • Automate Start Process: Immediately onboard allocated learners.
  • Reduce Waiting Time: Initiate learner participation from Day 1.
  • Budget Shift: Focus investment on Course programs.
  • Improve Reporting: Reclassify waiting users for accurate activity metrics.
