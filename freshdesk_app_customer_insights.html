Optimizing Customer Support Performance

Freshdesk Analytics & Workflow Enhancement

Introduction

This project analyzes Freshdesk support ticket data to evaluate operational efficiency, customer sentiment, and priority classification trends. The objective is to identify recurring support issues and workflow gaps affecting customer satisfaction.

Objective

  • Assess customer sentiment trends across support tickets.
  • Evaluate priority distribution and SLA pressure points.
  • Identify dominant support issue categories.
  • Improve support workflow through automation.

Data Cleaning and Preparation

  1. Processed ticket data for resolution time analysis.
  2. Categorized support issues by service type.
  3. Analyzed priority tagging distribution.
  4. Evaluated customer sentiment patterns.
  5. Structured ticket volume trends for performance monitoring.

Analysis & Key Findings

  • Average resolution time remained between 85–97 minutes.
  • Nearly 80% of tickets reflected negative sentiment.
  • Approximately 50% of tickets were marked as High Priority.
  • Billing and Login issues dominated ticket volume.
  • Early-week ticket peaks occurred on Mondays and Tuesdays.

Conclusion

Support workload remains stable, but recurring high-impact issues and priority imbalance are affecting customer satisfaction. Automation and workflow improvements are required to maintain service scalability.

Recommendations

  • Implement Macros: Automate recurring billing and login issue responses.
  • Smart Routing: Introduce automated ticket classification rules.
  • SLA Monitoring: Deploy escalation tracking systems.
  • Self-Service: Expand knowledge base support.

Summary

  • Tools Used Python, Tableau
  • Domain Customer Support Analytics
  • Skills Sentiment Analysis, SLA Monitoring, Workflow Optimization
